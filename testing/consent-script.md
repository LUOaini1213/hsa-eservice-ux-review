# Consent script (read aloud before recording)

"Thank you for helping. I'm testing two ways of finding information about bringing a medical device to market in Singapore: some public pages on the Health Sciences Authority website, and a prototype I built. I'm testing the designs, not you. There are no wrong answers, and if something is hard to find, that tells me the design needs work.

This is a personal project for my portfolio. It is not run by or for HSA.

The session takes about 45 minutes. I'll ask you to do three short tasks and think aloud while you work, then answer a few rating questions.

With your permission I'll record the screen and your voice. The recording stays on my own device, is not shared, and is deleted within 30 days of my analysis. In my notes you will be a code such as P3, not your name. Only anonymised results, such as times, success and ratings, may appear in my public repository.

You will not log in to anything, enter any personal information, or submit any form. If a page asks you to, stop and tell me.

You can skip any question, take a break, or stop at any time without giving a reason. If you stop, I'll delete your data if you ask.

Do you have any questions? Are you happy to take part? And are you happy for me to record the screen and audio?"
